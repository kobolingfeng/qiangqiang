# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

QiangQiang is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community. Participation is open regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and differing levels of experience.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Personal attacks, trolling, insults, or deliberately inflammatory conduct
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or project work
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `kobolingfeng@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation based on context, severity, and repeated behavior.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. False or malicious reports may themselves violate this code.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community codes of conduct and adapted for QiangQiang's current single-maintainer community.
